How RecLeague Works

Getting involved in recreational sports is simple.

STEP 1

Create Your Profile

Add your sport, skill level, location, and availability.

STEP 2

Find or Manage Teams

Players can find teams while team captains can manage rosters and open positions.

STEP 3

Connect and Play

Join teams, find substitutes, post games, and stay connected with your local sports community.
